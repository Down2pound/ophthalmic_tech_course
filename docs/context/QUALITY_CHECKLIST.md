# OptiTech Academy Publication Quality Checklist

Use this checklist for every lesson, assessment, scenario, job aid, certificate,
and substantial course revision.

## Accuracy and grounding

- [ ] Every clinical claim is supported by an approved source or reviewer.
- [ ] No medication direction, clinical threshold, device setting, or policy was
      invented.
- [ ] Facts supplied for the assignment were preserved accurately.
- [ ] Technical observations are distinguished from provider interpretation.
- [ ] Practice-specific content is labeled and does not masquerade as a
      universal rule.

## Safety, scope, and privacy

- [ ] No PHI or identifiable patient information appears.
- [ ] The technician's role and limits are clear.
- [ ] Stop, repeat, or escalate conditions are included where relevant.
- [ ] Hands-on procedures include a supervisor-observed competency requirement.
- [ ] Course completion is not represented as licensure, JCAHPO certification,
      or proof of independent clinical competency.

## Instructional quality

- [ ] The lesson states observable learning objectives.
- [ ] Every stated objective is actually taught.
- [ ] Terminology is defined before or when first used.
- [ ] The content is understandable to a new technician.
- [ ] Examples and scenarios are realistic, relevant, and de-identified.
- [ ] Common mistakes and prevention strategies are included.
- [ ] The content avoids unnecessary repetition and unrelated material.

## Assessment quality

- [ ] Every question maps to a stated objective.
- [ ] Each question has one clearly best answer unless otherwise marked.
- [ ] Distractors are plausible but unambiguously incorrect from the lesson.
- [ ] Correct and incorrect options include teaching explanations.
- [ ] Questions test useful knowledge rather than trivia or trick wording.
- [ ] Passing a quiz is not substituted for hands-on validation.

## Experience and publication

- [ ] Headings, lists, tables, and job aids are easy to scan.
- [ ] Patient-facing language is professional, respectful, and understandable.
- [ ] Links and downloadable resources work.
- [ ] Module numbering, prerequisites, and navigation are correct.
- [ ] Progress, quiz scoring, and completion behavior were tested.
- [ ] The owner, reviewer, version, and review date are recorded.
